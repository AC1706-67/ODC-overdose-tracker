## 📋 Recording Tips

### Before Recording:

- [ ] Charge your phone fully
- [ ] Clear notifications
- [ ] Have test data ready in the app
- [ ] Practice the flow 2-3 times
- [ ] Use a screen recorder (OBS, QuickTime, or phone screen recording)
- [ ] Test your microphone

### During Recording:

- Speak clearly and at a moderate pace
- Don't rush - 3-4 minutes is perfect
- If you mess up, just pause and restart that section
- Show confidence - you built something amazing!

### Editing:

- Use simple video editor (iMovie, DaVinci Resolve, or CapCut)
- Add title cards for each section
- Add background music (low volume, royalty-free)
- Export at 1080p

### Alternative: Loom or Screen Recording

If you don't want to edit:

- Use Loom.com (free, easy, records screen + webcam)
- Record in one take
- Share the link
